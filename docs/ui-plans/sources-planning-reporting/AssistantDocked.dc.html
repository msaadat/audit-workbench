NarrativeEdit

Condition

Two financial approval matrix entries carried approval limits below the maximum value of the requisitions approved by those authorities. The exceptions were:

AuthorityApproval limitHighest approved requisition
Head of Treasury1,000,0004,200,000
CFO10,000,00064,000,000

The Head of Treasury approved requisitions up to 4,200,000 against a limit of 1,000,000, and the CFO approved requisitions up to 64,000,000 against a limit of 10,000,000.

Criteria

The Delegation of Authority requires a commitment to be approved by an authority whose financial approval limit is equal to or above the committed value; approval by an authority below the committed value breaches the delegation.

Root cause

Pending auditor follow-up. The report will carry this section empty until a cause is recorded.Record the cause

Risk

Approvals exceeding the limits delegated to the approving officer expose the entity to commitments made outside the authority granted by the financial approval matrix.

Recommendation

Correct the financial approval matrix so that each authority's approval limit is set at or above the value of the requisitions that authority is permitted to approve, and review the requisitions already approved above the recorded limits to confirm they were approved under a validly delegated authority.
