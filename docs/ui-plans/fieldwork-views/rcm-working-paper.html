Working paper · RCM-FBB3A7

Approval authority against the Financial Approval Matrix

Purpose

To establish whether requisitions in the period were approved by an authority whose delegated limit covered the committed value, as required by section 3.2 of the Financial Approval Matrix.

Risk and control

Risk. Requisitions may be approved by an authority whose approval limit is below the committed value.

Control. The designated Financial Authority approves the committed value against the Financial Approval Matrix before the requisition is released to Procurement. Rated High; preventive; owned by the Financial Authority.

Procedures performed

One data test was run over the full requisition population (64 records) comparing each approver's delegated limit with the requisition's estimated cost.

TestProcedurePopulationResult
DAT-88A1C2Approver limit is at least the requisition value64 requisitions2 exceptions

Results

Two requisitions (REQ2024017, REQ2024041) were approved by an authority whose delegated limit was below the estimated cost. Both remain open exceptions; neither has been accepted on review.

Conclusion

The control is assessed as ineffective for the period. Finding F-0571DE (critical) is drafted from these exceptions.
