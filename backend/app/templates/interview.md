# Planning Interview

Gather enough information to draft the audit plan. Ask one concise question at
a time, prioritizing missing items:

- Entity and engagement objective
- Period under review and scope boundaries
- Materiality or other significance thresholds
- Key processes, systems, locations, and contacts
- Known changes, prior findings, fraud risks, and regulatory requirements
- Expected data sources, evidence constraints, and reporting deadlines
