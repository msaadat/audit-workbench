The architectural moat

The model sees the map. Never the records.

Traditional AI analysis sends data outward. Audit Workbench reverses the flow: the model receives metadata, aggregate statistics and safe previews, then calls tightly governed tools that execute locally.

The result is useful agentic analysis with a defensible data boundary—and a visible, editable Polars escape hatch when a standard procedure is not enough.

The working surfaces

Automation without giving up the auditor's tools.

01 · Query

Shape the evidence without writing code.

Auditors can move from row-level inspection to filters, groups, aggregations and cross-tabs in one visual canvas. Every change recomputes locally and can be exported or pinned as a live dashboard view.

Why it matters: ad-hoc exploration becomes a repeatable analysis specification—not a trail of spreadsheet manipulations.
02 · Validation

Turn data understanding into reusable controls.

The agent proposes profile-based rules; the auditor can inspect and edit every check—from required fields and value domains to ranges, patterns and compound keys—before running them.

Why it matters: suggested controls are explicit, persistent and reviewable. They can be rerun whenever refreshed engagement data arrives.
03 · Analysis

Keep the conclusion attached to its evidence.

Saved procedures carry their parameters, verdict, statistics, summary and detail rows together. Auditors can choose from the library, inspect agent-created work, write custom Polars, export results or promote them to the dashboard.

Why it matters: the agent leaves behind durable working assets—not an answer trapped inside a chat transcript.
