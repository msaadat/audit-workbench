Agentic execution

One instruction. The engagement gets built.

From the assistant panel, a single request — “Prepare a full audit working draft” — planned and executed a 60-task run in this engagement: reading the SOP and minutes, drafting the APM and RCM, preparing tests, creating findings and generating working papers and the report draft.

01
Two working modes

Auto applies validated changes; Ask pauses at editable approval batches.

02
Steerable, durable runs

Pause, resume, redirect or cancel — progress and history survive restarts.

03
Honest status

Issues are reported, not hidden — completed work stays clearly separated from open items.

The assistant panel keeps the run, its tasks and its results beside the work itself.
